# Little Giant · Trailer & demo script

## 1. Trailer (83 s, 4K 30 fps)

Rendered live in the game engine, then finished with title cards and a sound mix.

```
node tools/run.mjs tools/trailer.ts          # footage, 3840x2160 (~80 s)
node tools/run.mjs tools/trailer-finish.ts   # title cards, sound mix, encode (~8 min)
# -> tools/.out/trailer/little-giant-trailer-4k.mp4, little-giant-trailer-1080p.mp4
```

## 2. Live demo script (about 4 min, on an iPhone)

**Before the demo:**
- Release build installed.
- Save reset, or a save at around level 5 with a few skill points to spend.
- Phone in landscape, sound on.
- Deep links:
  - `studio.vgang.battle://?explore=1` goes straight into the valley.
  - `studio.vgang.battle://?explore=1&brawl=3` starts the auto-fight stress test.

### 0:00 · Title (20 s)
- **Show:** the menu. The valley at golden hour drifts behind it.
- **Say:** "Little Giant, a small hero's journey across Việt Nam. Everything you see is rendered live on the phone, in React Native, with WebGPU."
- **Highlight:** the menu background is the real game world, not a video.

### 0:20 · The valley (50 s)
- **Do:** tap EXPLORE NINH BÌNH. Walk SORA out of the village, turn the camera round, sprint along the river.
- **Say:** "This is Tràng An, in Ninh Bình: karst towers, rice terraces, bamboo, lotus on the river. The buildings are Vietnamese: the thủy đình on the water, the pagoda, with its signs in Vietnamese."
- **Highlights:**
  - The gold arrows streaming along the ground to the next quest. The beacon over the shrine.
  - Real shadows under SORA and the monsters. Clean edges (4× MSAA). The river mirroring the pagoda.
  - The FPS counter in the corner: 60.

### 1:10 · The fight (70 s)
- **Do:** walk up to a pack; they spot SORA ("!") and rush her.
  - Slash combo (tap), dash through an attack, streak (skill), kiếm khí from range.
  - Guard at the last moment to parry.
- **Say:** "Monsters roam in packs and hunt you on sight. Every blow has weight: hit-stop, sparks, knock-backs."
- **Highlights:**
  - The combo counter.
  - Damage numbers.
  - The kiếm khí bending toward its target and piercing three monsters.

### 2:20 · The ultimates (40 s)
- **Do:** fill the ring once and fire the Heaven Pierce. Then fill it twice (the button turns pink, ×2) and fire Sen Bão.
- **Say:** "Charge your energy once for the Heaven Pierce. Hold it, charge it twice, and it becomes Sen Bão, the lotus tempest."
- **Highlight:** the lotus opening on the ground, the petals whirling up, and the frame rate holding.

### 3:00 · Growing (30 s)
- **Do:** level up (or open the portrait if points are waiting). Spend a point in Vitality and show the HP going up.
- **Say:** "Every level brings a skill point, and one more every fifth level. Six skills, five ranks each."

### 3:30 · How it is built (30 s)
- **Say:** "No game engine. One React Native app:
  - three.js on WebGPU, with shaders written in TypeScript through TypeGPU.
  - The world is built in Blender, with pieces generated by Meshy at 4K, compressed to ASTC.
  - The UI is plain React Native and Reanimated, over the game."
- **Optional:** open `brawl=3` and let the auto-fight run with three packs at once, pointing at the FPS counter.

### Talking points if asked
- **Performance:** GPU-driven plants (one draw per kind, culled on the GPU), frozen static matrices, the reflection at 30 Hz. About 9 ms of JS per frame in a three-pack fight.
- **Crispness:** 4× MSAA scene pass, depth-based ink outlines, a far-distance blur, 16× anisotropic ASTC textures.
- **Content pipeline:** Blender builds the valley.
  - Meshy handles the concept image, then Meshy 7 image-to-3D with a 4K texture.
  - Vietnamese signs are rendered into an atlas and laid onto the models.
